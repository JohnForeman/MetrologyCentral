## Books

<!-- TODO: once Amazon Associates is approved, append &tag=YOURTAG-20 to each Amazon link (or swap in SiteStripe links). -->

- **Fundamentals of Dimensional Metrology** (Connie Dotson): a classic textbook on measurement fundamentals. [Find on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Fundamentals+of+Dimensional+Metrology+Dotson)
- **The Metrology Handbook** (ASQ Measurement Quality Division, ed. Jay L. Bucher): calibration, uncertainty and lab management. [Find on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=The+Metrology+Handbook+Bucher)
- **Coordinate Measuring Machines and Systems** (Hocken &amp; Pereira): an in-depth reference on CMM technology. [Find on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Coordinate+Measuring+Machines+and+Systems+Hocken)
- **Fundamentals of Geometric Dimensioning and Tolerancing** (Alex Krulikowski): a widely used GD&amp;T text. [Find on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/s?k=Fundamentals+of+Geometric+Dimensioning+and+Tolerancing+Krulikowski)
- **Measurement Systems Analysis (MSA) reference manual**: published by AIAG, sold directly at [aiag.org](https://www.aiag.org/).
